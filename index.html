How reliable is this

  • The El Niño itself is confirmed and very strong. That is observation, not modelling.
  • Peak and decay: the peak is expected November–January. The timing of the return to neutral (spring–summer 2027) and the chance of La Niña in late 2027 come from analogues; the agencies have not stated them yet.
  • Regional impacts are probabilistic. In 2015–16 California expected heavy rain and mostly did not get it. Three dots mean "almost always", one means "sometimes".
  • The Niño-3.4 curve is schematic: there is one anchor point (September, +2.1 °C from CPC), the rest is interpolation and a forecast spread.
  • Outcomes of seasons that are already over, such as the 2026 Indian monsoon, are not checked against observations here. The page shows the historical risk.
