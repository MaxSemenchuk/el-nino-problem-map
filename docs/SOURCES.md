# Sources

Collected from public web pages on 2026-10-30. Search summaries were used to locate these pages; the figures below were **not** independently checked against the primary documents. Anything that depends on a number is marked `VERIFY` in the data.

## Event status and forecast

- NOAA Climate Prediction Center, ENSO Diagnostic Discussion, monthly bulletins 2026. Latest found: 10 September 2026. https://www.cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.shtml
  - Reported: El Niño Advisory in effect; 75% chance of a historic event (stronger than any since 1950) for October–December 2026. Earlier bulletins gave other figures (for example 81% very strong in July, over 90% in August). The October bulletin was not found. `VERIFY`
- WMO, El Niño/La Niña Update, May and August 2026, and the 3 September 2026 news release "El Niño set to become very strong, raising risks of extreme weather into 2027". https://wmo.int/node/38943 · https://wmo.int/resources/publication-series/el-ninola-nina-updates/august-2026
  - Reported: El Niño established, nearly 100% odds of persisting through February 2027, no La Niña expected in the outlook period, strength does not map directly onto impact size everywhere, Indian Ocean Dipole interaction matters.
- CVF/V20 Science Bulletins 01–03 on El Niño (May, July, August 2026). https://cvfv20.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/CVF-V20-Science-Bulletin-03__El-Nino.pdf

## Humanitarian and anticipatory action

- FAO and WFP, Joint Anticipatory Action Appeal, June 2026 to March 2027: USD 202 million, about 8.8 million people, 22 countries, launched 18 June 2026. https://www.wfp.org/publications/el-nino-fao-wfp-joint-anticipatory-action-appeal-june-2026-march-2027 · https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/bracing-for-el-ni%C3%B1o--fao-and-wfp-launch-joint-appeal-to-protect-8.8-million-people-from-extreme-weather-events/en
  - A later summary cites a funding gap of USD 167 million. `VERIFY`
- WFP, "World Food Programme ramps up preparedness and response plans as El Niño threatens to push tens of millions into hunger". https://www.wfp.org/news/world-food-programme-ramps-preparedness-and-response-plans-el-nino-threatens-push-tens
  - Reported: at least 49 million more people in acute food insecurity by the end of 2027; anticipatory plans triggered since May in South Sudan, Chad, Mauritania, Uganda, Guatemala and El Salvador; the 2015–16 event affected 60 to 100 million people. `VERIFY`
- Anticipation Hub, "El Niño 2026–2027: seizing the moment". https://www.anticipation-hub.org/news/el-nino-2026-2027-seizing-the-moment
  - Source for: barriers (financing, triggers and readiness, coordination, forecasts and data, local actors, multi-sector scope), the Anticipatory Action Task Force membership, frameworks in about 75 countries, up to 1.7 percentage points of GDP at risk in vulnerable countries.
- Welthungerhilfe, El Niño potential implications briefing, June 2026. https://www.welthungerhilfe.org/fileadmin/pictures/publications/en/fact-sheets/topics/2026-june-el-nino-potential-implications-briefing-whh.pdf
- PreventionWeb, "El Niño impacts on food security: lessons from past El Niño events for 2026/27 preparedness" (June 2026). https://www.preventionweb.net/quick/102957
- Norad, "Preventing the worst impacts of El Niño" (2026). https://www.norad.no/en/news/news/2026/preventing-the-worst-impacts-of-el-nino/

## Regional outlook

- FEWS NET, El Niño 2026–2027 topic page. https://fews.net/topics/el-nino-2026-2027
  - Reported: high food-insecurity concern for October 2026 to March 2027 in southern Africa; disruption of rains in East and the Horn of Africa; below-average rain in South and Southeast Asia.
- BMI via Malay Mail, 26 October 2026, on Southeast Asian crop and power risks. https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/2026/10/26/bmi-malaysia-among-south-east-asian-economies-facing-el-nino-crop-power-risks/240704
  - Reported: rice, Thai sugar and palm oil are drought-exposed; Philippines, Thailand and Malaysia most at risk. `VERIFY`

## Not yet researched

## Open-source projects (GitHub scan, 2026-10-30)

Found by searching GitHub topics (`anticipatory-action`, `el-nino`, `humanitarian-data`) and keywords. Only READMEs and repository metadata were read, not the code. Several are one-person prototypes. A project's link to a problem is the author's reading of its stated purpose.

- El Niño Atlas: https://github.com/AlKhourdajie/El_Nino_Atlas (MIT; tracks this event across forecast, anticipatory action and realised impact)
- Red Cross IBF system and pipelines: https://github.com/rodekruis/IBF-system (Apache 2.0)
- ForeAct (Fiji): https://github.com/tom231826-svg/foreact (MIT; working prototype on illustrative data)
- AA CoPilot: https://github.com/nitinmagima/AA_CoPilot (MIT)
- Anticipa: https://github.com/gabrielmussafiri/Anticipa (no licence; early prototype)
- Deliberation enablers, not El Niño specific: Polis https://github.com/compdemocracy/polis (AGPL-3.0), Talk to the City https://github.com/AIObjectives/tttc-light-js (Apache 2.0), Decidim https://github.com/decidim/decidim and Loomio https://github.com/loomio/loomio (AGPL-3.0)

Other El Niño repositories seen but not added (forecasting and visualisation prototypes): CAS-Canglong, kmd-labs/el-nino, Godzila-El-Nino.
